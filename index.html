About me

I'm a 25-year-old junior front-end developer who enjoys building responsive, user-friendly interfaces. I like turning ideas into clean, intuitive websites and writing code that's simple and easy to maintain. I have a degree in Computer Science with a background in cybersecurity, which has given me a strong eye for detail and an interest in secure coding practices.
